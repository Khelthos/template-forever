## How it went

On Tuesday evening we formed three groups of five players. Each group had at least one experienced member who explained the encounters before pulling.

| Group | Composition | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | Warrior, Priest, Mage, Rogue, Hunter | Cleared |
| Group 2 | Paladin, Druid, Warlock, Mage, Hunter | Cleared |
| Group 3 | Warrior, Shaman, Rogue, Warlock, Priest | Cleared on the second attempt |

## What we learned

1. Talking before each pull prevents most wipes.
2. Controlling extra enemies is essential for less experienced groups.
3. A five-minute break every hour keeps everyone focused.

Thanks to everyone who joined: see you next Tuesday!
