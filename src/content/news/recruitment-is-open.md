## A new adventure begins

With new content on the way we want to grow the roster and form a second group for evening dungeons. Whether you are just starting out or a seasoned veteran, there is a place for you.

## Who we are looking for

- Players who are active at least two evenings a week.
- A cooperative attitude and willingness to learn.
- Healers and tanks of any class.

> "A guild isn't measured by its gear, but by how many times it gets back up together."

## How to apply

You'll find every contact on [The guild](../../about/#contacts) page. An officer will get back to you within a few days.
