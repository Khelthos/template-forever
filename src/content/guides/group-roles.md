An introductory **demo** guide to group roles.

## Tank

The tank draws the enemies' attention and protects the rest of the group. They set the pace of the run and the order in which enemies are engaged.

## Healer

The healer keeps the group alive. They watch everyone's health, anticipate incoming damage and manage their resources carefully.

## DPS

DPS players deal most of the damage. Their job is to defeat enemies in the agreed order without pulling attention onto themselves.

## How to choose

Try different roles in guild dungeons: ask an officer and we'll pair you with an experienced member.
