A **demo** editorial guide with generic organization tips that work for any guild.

## Before the evening

1. Confirm your attendance in the guild calendar.
2. Read the guide for the encounters we will face.
3. Repair your gear and prepare your consumables.

## During the raid

### Communication

- A single officer gives the calls during fights.
- Keep voice chat clear during encounters.
- Report connection problems right away.

### Breaks

We take a short break every hour. Use it to grab some water and stretch your legs.

## After the raid

Share your impressions in the dedicated channel: what worked and what we can improve.
