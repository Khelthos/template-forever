## Our story

Stormwatch started as a small group of friends who met every evening to run dungeons together. Over time the group grew, and today it brings together players with different experience and schedules, united by the wish to explore Azeroth in good company.

## Philosophy and values

- **Respect first:** no tolerance for toxic behavior, inside or outside the guild.
- **Helping each other:** experienced players guide those who are still learning.
- **Sustainable commitment:** the game stays a pleasure, not a second job.

## Organization

| Rank | Role in the guild |
| --- | --- |
| Guild Master | Leads the guild and makes the final decisions |
| Officer | Organizes raids, dungeons and recruitment |
| Veteran | Experienced members and a reference for everyone else |
| Member | Full members of the guild |
| Recruit | Two-week trial period |

## Requirements

We don't ask for a minimum item level. We expect:

1. Respectful behavior towards everyone.
2. Punctuality at the events you sign up for.
3. Willingness to learn the strategies before raids.

## How to apply

Reach out through the channels listed on this page: an officer will answer and set up a first dungeon run together so we can get to know each other.
